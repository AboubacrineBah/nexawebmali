Afrique Artisanat

Jira porozɛ

Mali ka artisanat ye e-commerce sojɛ

Online sojɛ dafalen (site, klient yɔrɔ ni back-office) Laravel ye jɔra, walasa ka an ka dɔnni fɔlɔw bonya ani komandi ɲini nɔgɔya.

Klient:
Afrique Artisanat (fɛn-fɔlɔ).
Baara sektor:
Artisanat ni feereya
San:
2026.
Janya:
Kalo fila.
Baara:
UX/UI jɔli, back-end ni front-end jɔli, testw, sɛbɛnni.
Teknolojiyɛ:
Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap 5, Vite, PHPUnit.

Sɛbɛnni

Mali ni West Afriki ka artisanw caman (daakɛnw, bijuw, walew, fɛn fɛnsɛnw, kanw) bɛ fɛn feere sugu la ani butik la, online u ka dɔnni tɔɔrɔ. Afrique Artisanat ye sojɛ fɛn-fɔlɔ ye min bɛ o ɲinini jaabi: ka baaraw jira, ka fɛn kelen-kelen jɛ artisan kelen ye, ani ka komandiw sɔrɔ online. O ye NexaWeb Mali ka jira porozɛ fana ye: Laravel aplikasyon dafalen, sɛbɛnnen ni tesira, min bɛ se ka sigi local XAMPP la.

Gɛlɛya

Feere taamali nɔgɔman, telefɔni kosɔn: Page minnu kalan nɔgɔ ekiran fitini kan, ani komandi kɛ ekiran dɔgɔ la.

Sara cogo min ka ɲi yɔrɔ in na, nka wari yɛrɛ tɛ ta: Sara fɛn sera waati, Orange Money ni Moov Money ye fɔlɔ simulation ye, nka kɛcogo bɛ labɛn baara yɛrɛ ye.

Feere kunnafoniw ɲuman: Fɛn min tɛ stock la tɛ feere; sara ni adɛsi bɛ mara komandi kɛ waati cogo la, kɔrɔfɔli ma yɛlɛma.

Teknoloji min ka ɲi yɔrɔ in na: XAMPP (MariaDB, PHP version), Bootstrap ni Laravel, Vite ni Sass bɛ styles jɔ.

Ɲɛnabɔliw

Laravel (MVC): Tables 9, controllers 21, Form Requests ye seko sɛgɛsɛgɛ, ani klient kelen-kelen bɛ se ka a yɛrɛ ka komandiw dɔrɔn lajɛ.

Baara kɛcogo bɛ services la: Panie bɛ session la; komandi bɛ transaction la sɛbɛn (stock lajɛra ka dɔgɔya), sara cogo bɛ interface kɔfɛ.

Komandi ka taama: A bɛ sabali, A sɔnnina, A ci, A sera. I bɛ se k’a bɔ ci waati, fɛn bɛ segi stock la.

Bootstrap 5 interface: Sass thème site ka jɛɲɛgɛnw, Blade vues 48 ni formu minnu bɛ se ka baara kɛ tuma wɛrɛ.

Kɛcogo ɲuman ni jɔli: Test automatiques 25, GitHub Actions continuous integration, porozɛ kunnafoni sɛbɛn ni maquette 50 (page 25 ordinatɛri ni telefɔni ye).

Nafolo ɲɛtaa

  • Fɛn feerew lajɛ min bɛ filɛ (kategori, sara, fɛn kɛcogo, stock, lajɛ cogo), panie ni komandi ekiran 3 la, ni ɲɛtaa ye.
  • Klient yɔrɔ (komandiw, profil, adɛsiw) ni back-office dafalen (fɛn feerew, komandiw, klientw, batakiw).
  • Fɛn feere 18, kategori 6 ni jira baarakɛyɔrɔ 6, ni test comptew ye.
  • Test automatiques 25 ni kunnafoni dafalen walasa ka porozɛ sigi local.
  • Loading waati, Lighthouse scores ni test laban: olu bɛna sɛgɛsɛgɛ porozɛ sigi kɔfɛ.
