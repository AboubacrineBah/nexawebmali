Sɛbɛnni

NexaWeb Mali ye web jɔli baara ye min sigira Bamako la, freelance ye daminɛ, ani a b’a fɛ ka kɛ dijitali agence ye min bɛ Afriki ni diɲɛ bɛɛ ka klientw ye. Site in ka fɔlɔ ɲinini ye ka portfolio bila online walasa front-end dɔnni jira.

Gɛlɛya

Ka NexaWeb Mali ka kɛ ko ɲuman ye TPE/PME, agencew (dɛmɛbaara) ni startupw ɲɛ.

Ka cifɔli ɲininiw sɔrɔ, Mali ni yɔrɔ wɛrɛw la (Malt, Upwork).

Dɔnni jira ni site yɛrɛ ka kɛcogo ye: design ɲuman, teliya, sekoɲɔgɔnyalen. Ka Bootstrap fɛn sigi walasa k’u baara kɛ klient site nataw la.

Ɲɛnabɔliw

Site min bɛ baara jira, Bootstrap thème yɛrɛ ta, ja nafolo dɔgɔw ani teknologi taamali nɔgɔman responsive.
