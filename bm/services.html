Web baaraw i ka bonya ka ɲɛtaa waati bɛɛ

NexaWeb Mali bɛ baarakɛyɔrɔ site la ka taa aplikasyon jɔli ma, ka site da, tekniki jɔli ni ɲɛnabɔli kɛ. Baara yɔrɔ saba, kɛcogo ɲuman kelen.

/

BUILD baara yɔrɔ — I ka online sigiyɔrɔ da

Site min bɛ i ka baara jira walima online sojɛ min bɛ kɛnɛya di, bɛ teliya, ani bɛ baara kɛ telefɔni ni ordinatɛri kan. WordPress ye, i yɛrɛ bɛ se ka i ka kɔnɔkow yɛlɛma.

  • WordPress sitew baara ɲuman: Site ɲuman walasa ka i ka baara, fɛn feerew ni kunnafoni jira.
  • E-commerce: Online sojɛ walasa ka feere Bamako kɔrɔ, ni fɛn feerew list min ka ɲɛnabɔ nɔgɔ.
  • Responsive interfacew: Page minnu bɛ yɛlɛma ka ɲɛ telefɔni ni ekiranba bɛɛ kan.
  • A ka ɲi: seginkɛlaw, artisanw, baara baarakɛlaw ni jɛkulu minnu.
  • Teknolojiyɛ: WordPress · WooCommerce · HTML5 · CSS3 · Bootstrap · Laravel.
  • Sara: porozɛ kunnafoni sɛbɛnni kɔfɛ.
Cifɔli ɲini
  • React web aplikasyonw: Aplikasyon kelen-page teliw minnu bɛ kuma ni baarakɛla ye.
  • Front-end jɔli: Interfacew nɔgɔman, sekoɲɔgɔnyalen ani i ka tɔgɔ-cogo ɲɛ.
  • PHP back-end jɔli: Data basew, baara kɛcogo ani faraliw i ka ɲinini kɔrɔ
  • API ni jɛɲɔgɔnyali: I ka site walima aplikasyon jɛ ni i ka baarakɛfɛnw wɛrɛw ye
  • I yɛrɛ ka web ɲɛnabɔli: I ka ɲinini jɔnjɔn bɛ? An bɛ o lajɛ, ka ɲɛnabɔli dɔ sigi a ye.
  • A ka ɲi: startupw, porozɛ jɔlaw, agencew ni baarakɛlaw minnu ka ɲinini jɔnjɔn bɛ.
  • Teknolojiyɛ: React · JavaScript · PHP · MySQL · Laravel
  • Sara: Don kelen ka sara walima porozɛ kunnafoni sɛbɛnni kɔfɛ.

ADVANCED baara yɔrɔ — I yɛrɛ ka aplikasyonw jɔ

Ni modello dɔ tɛ se ka dafa: baara aplikasyonw, interfacew ni jɛɲɔgɔnyali minnu jɔra i ka sigida ye. An bɛ interface jɔ ni React ye, serveur fan ni PHP ye.

↻

RUN baara yɔrɔ — Ka i ka site teli ani kɛnɛya la

Online site b’a fɛ ka ɲɛnabɔ: kura ni, kunnafoni mara, kɛnɛya, teliya. Ni i bɛ sara kalo kalo, ni gɛlɛya bɛ, i bɛ se ka kuma ni baarakɛla ye ani dusu ka hɛrɛ.

  • WordPress ɲɛnabɔli ni kɛnɛya: fɛn kura, kunnafoni mara, kɛnɛya ni gɛlɛya ɲɛnabɔ.
  • Teliya ɲɛnabɔ: Teliya lajɛ ni ɲɛnabɔli walasa pagew ka teli.
  • A ka ɲi: baarakɛlaw minnu bɛ site dɔ la, b’a fɛ k’a kɛ ɲuman ani k’a kɛnɛya.
  • Teknolojiyɛ: WordPress · PHP · MySQL.
  • Sara: Kalo kalo sara: don kelen ka sara.
    Gɛlɛya ɲɛnabɔ walima lajɛ kelen-kelen: don sara walima lɛrɛ sara.
Cifɔli ɲini

Teknolojiyɛ minnu sɛgɛsɛgɛra, i ka ɲinini ye olu sugandi

WordPress React PHP HTML5 CSS3 Bootstrap Laravel

WordPress ni WooCommerce walasa ka baara da teli ani ka i yɛrɛ se. React interfacew minnu bɛ kuma baarakɛla ye. PHP ni MySQL serveur kɛcogo ni data basew ye. HTML5, CSS3 ni Bootstrap page nafolojuw, ekiran bɛɛ kan.

Cifɔli nɔgɔman, fɛn kelen-kelen

Porozɛ kelen-kelen bɛ yɛrɛ. Fɔ i ka porozɛ kunna: i bɛ cifɔli sɔrɔ ni waati ni baara kelen-kelen ka sara ye.

Fɛn minnu bɛ sara yɛlɛma Page hakɛ, fɛn kɛcogo (sojɛ, reservation, mɔgɔw ka yɔrɔ…), kɔnɔkow hakɛ min bɛ don, ni site kanw.
Cifɔli ɲini

Ɲininkali minnu bɛ kɛ waati bɛɛ

Sara bɛ i ka porozɛ la. An bɛna cifɔli kunnafoni fɔ i ye, baara kelen-kelen.

A bɛ porozɛ ka bonya la. Cifɔli bɛ waati fɔ baara kelen-kelen ye. Site min bɛ baara jira: siman 2 ka taa 3, porozɛ ka gɛlɛya cogo la.

An bɛna 40 ka taa 50% ɲini porozɛ sɛbɛnni waati (Mali ka porozɛw kosɔn), kɔfɛ saraw bɛ kɛ baara ka ɲɛtaa cogo la porozɛ janw kosɔn.

WordPress bɛ se ka site da teli, ani i bɛ i ka kɔnɔkow ɲɛnabɔ i yɛrɛ. React ka ɲi aplikasyon minnu bɛ kuma baarakɛla ye. An bɛna baarakɛfɛn min ka ɲi i ka ɲinini ye, o lase i ma, tɛ fɛn gɛlɛn.

Ɔwɔ. An bɛ site kelen-kelen daminɛ telefɔni kan jɔli la, kɔfɛ an bɛ k’a ɲɛnabɔ tablet ni ordinatɛri kan.

Ɔwɔ, WordPress ye: an bɛna jira i la i bɛ se ka sɛbɛnw, jaw ni fɛn feerew yɛlɛma cogo.

Ɔwɔ, kalo kalo sara ye: fɛn kura, kunnafoni mara, kɛnɛya ni gɛlɛya ɲɛnabɔ.

Ɔwɔ, telebaara la. An ka GMT waati bɛ baara ɲɛnabɔ ni Europe ni yɔrɔ wɛrɛw ka ekipuw ye.

Ɔwɔ. An bɛ se ka baara kɛ sariya la i ka ɲinini jɔnjɔn kosɔn: thème i yɛrɛ ka, jɛɲɔgɔnyali gɛlɛn, PHP ɲɛnabɔli, baara ka bonya waati.

Porozɛ dɔ bɛ i dusu la wa? An ye kuma o la.
