Afrique Artisanat

Demo project

E-commerce store for Malian crafts

A complete online store (website, customer area, and admin dashboard) built with Laravel to showcase local craftsmanship and simplify ordering.

Client:
Afrique Artisanat (fictional).
Industry:
Crafts and retail
Year:
2026.
Duration:
2 months.
Roles:
UX/UI design, front-end and back-end development, testing, and documentation.
Technologies:
Laravel, PHP, MySQL, Bootstrap 5, Vite, PHPUnit.

Context

Many Malian and West African artisans (textiles, jewelry, sculpture, leather goods, and pottery) sell mainly in markets and shops, with limited online visibility. Afrique Artisanat is a fictional store designed to meet this need: showcase creations, connect each product with its artisan, and accept orders online. It is also a NexaWeb Mali demo project: a complete, documented, and tested Laravel application that can be installed locally with XAMPP.

Challenges

A simple shopping journey designed for mobile first: easy-to-read pages on small screens and an order process completed in just a few steps.

Payment options suited to the local context, with no real transactions: simulated cash on delivery, Orange Money, and Moov Money, with logic ready for real services.

Reliable sales data: no overselling, prices and addresses preserved as submitted with each order, and a complete order history.

A stack suited to the local environment: XAMPP (MariaDB and PHP version), Bootstrap with Laravel, with styles compiled using Vite and Sass.

Solutions

Laravel (MVC): 9 tables, 21 controllers, validation with Form Requests, and each customer limited to their own orders.

Business logic in services: session-based cart, orders saved in a transaction (stock checked and then decremented), and payment gateways behind an interface.

Order lifecycle: Pending, Confirmed, Shipped, and Delivered, with cancellation and restocking allowed before shipment.

Bootstrap 5 interface: a Sass theme matching the site colors, 48 Blade views, and reusable form components.

Quality and design: 25 automated tests, GitHub Actions continuous integration, project requirements, and 50 mockups (25 pages in desktop and mobile versions).

Results

  • A filterable catalog (category, price, material, stock, and sorting), a shopping cart, and a three-step order flow with tracking
  • A customer area (orders, profile, addresses) and a complete admin dashboard (products, orders, customers, messages)
  • 18 products, 6 categories, and 6 demo workshops, with test accounts
  • 25 automated tests and complete documentation for local installation
  • Load times, Lighthouse scores, and acceptance testing: to be measured after installation
