A web studio born in Bamako, built for the world

NexaWeb Mali builds modern websites, applications, and digital solutions for businesses, entrepreneurs, and organizations.

Our story

NexaWeb Mali was founded on a simple belief: Malian businesses deserve world-class digital tools. The work began in Bamako as an independent web development practice. The ambition is to build a strong reputation among small and medium-sized businesses and entrepreneurs, then grow into a digital agency serving clients across Africa and around the world.

A name that captures the vision

Nexa | Connection, next-generation thinking, innovation, and an international outlook.

Web | Our craft: web development.

Mali | Our local roots, African identity, and starting point.

Vision and mission

To become a leading web engineering studio in West Africa and demonstrate that Malian talent can compete globally.
To make advanced technology more accessible to local small businesses and startups.

Our values
